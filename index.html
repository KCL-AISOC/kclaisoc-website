Who We Are

Beyond Stocks
and Bonds

KCL AISOC is King's College London's only society dedicated exclusively to alternative asset management. We cover the private markets that move the world: private equity, infrastructure, hedge funds, real assets, private credit, and venture capital. Founded in April 2026, we exist to bridge the gap between academic study and the alternative investment industry.
