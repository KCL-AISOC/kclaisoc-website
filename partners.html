Connect your organisation with King's College London's most finance-focused alternative investment talent.

Reach the Students Who Choose Alternative Assets

KCL AISOC exists at the intersection of academic rigour and industry ambition. Our members are not passively studying finance. They are actively seeking careers in private equity, hedge funds, infrastructure, real assets, private credit, and venture capital.

King's College London ranks among the UK's top universities for graduate employability, with 42,000 students across six faculties. Our members are drawn from finance, economics, mathematics, computer science, and law, the academic backgrounds that feed directly into alternative investment careers.

A corporate partnership with KCL AISOC gives your organisation direct, high-signal access to this audience through events, content, and branded engagement channels that cut through the noise of campus recruitment.

Three Terms.
Year-Round Presence.

The KCL AISOC programme runs across all three terms, giving corporate partners consistent, scheduled touchpoints with members throughout the academic year.

Autumn Term October to December
  • Society launch and member induction
  • Freshers Fair recruitment campaign
  • Inaugural speaker event, featuring an industry professional
  • Introduction to alternative investments workshop
  • First partner campus engagement opportunity
  • Autumn investment report published
Spring Term January to March
  • Annual Investment Summit (Founding Partner naming rights)
  • Industry Speaker Series, a multi-session programme
  • Investment case competition, a deal structuring challenge
  • Spring newsletter campaign with partner profiles
  • CV drop and application support for partner firms
  • Spring investment report published
Summer Term April to June
  • End-of-year networking evening
  • Internship and summer programme promotion to members
  • Annual partner review and next-year strategy meetings
  • Summer investment report and year-in-review publication
  • Next committee recruitment and institutional knowledge handover
  • Renewal and new partnership enquiries open
