Spring Week Panel

Wednesday 7 October 2026, 6.30pm to 8pm. Room NE 1.03, Bush House, Strand Campus, King's College London.

About the Panel

Event details

Six KCL students who have completed spring weeks talked through what the week actually looks like day to day, what doing well involves, and how they approached networking. The panel was aimed at students thinking about applying for a spring week, with time to chat with the speakers afterwards.
