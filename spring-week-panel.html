Spring Week Panel

Wednesday 7 October 2026, 6.30pm to 8pm. Room NE 1.03, Bush House, Strand Campus, King's College London.

Sign up
About the Panel

What to expect

Seven KCL students who have completed spring weeks talk through what the week actually looks like day to day, what doing well involves, and how they approached networking. The panel is aimed at students who are thinking about applying for a spring week. There will be time to chat with the speakers afterwards.
